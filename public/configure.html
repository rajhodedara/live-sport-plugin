🏆 Nuvio Collections (Replay Hubs)

Get dedicated Landscape cards on your Nuvio home screen for each sport (like Netflix & Prime Video). Clicking any sport opens organized match replay rows (Recent, Premier League, NBA, F1, etc.)!

📥 Download JSON
How to import in Nuvio:
1. Click Copy JSON or Download JSON.
2. In Nuvio app, open Settings ➔ Collections ➔ Import Collection.
3. Paste the JSON or select the downloaded file.
4. Enjoy Netflix-style sport replay hubs on your home screen!
